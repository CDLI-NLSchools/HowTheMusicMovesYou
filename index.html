How The Music Moves You

Grade 9 STEAM, Beyond Our Planet, Lesson 8: Music and Space (Extension Activity)

Listening to music can evoke an emotional response. In this activity you will watch and listen to one song closely, build a personal soundtrack of five songs that represent who you are, and create an illustration for one of them. Your journal stays on this page: nothing is sent anywhere. When you finish, print or copy your journal to keep it with your work.

Part 1: What does it all mean?

First, watch the video for "Rocket Man" by Elton John. The video is an animated film created by filmmaker Majid Adin. Listen and watch closely, and notice the feelings and emotions that the song and the images bring up for you.

Watch "Rocket Man" by Elton John (opens in a new tab)

After listening to and watching the video, use the sticky notes below to list at least 3 feelings and/or emotions that you experienced. Put one feeling or emotion on each note. For example, you might feel happy, excited, or intrigued: your notes will be your own.

Part 2: Sounds that move you!

List and explain the five songs that would make up your personal soundtrack. Really think about what represents who you are and what you are about. These songs might not necessarily be your favourite songs, but they should be songs that represent who you are.

Part 3: Art imitates life

Now that you have had a chance to explore Rocket Man, as well as your own personal soundtrack, it is time to express yourself. Select one of the five songs that you listed in Part 2 and illustrate it. You can choose to create several illustrations that represent different parts of the song, or you can choose to create one illustration which represents the entire piece. You can choose any medium you would like: digital, paint, pastel, pencil crayon, and so on. The drawing pad below is one way to create your illustration right here.
