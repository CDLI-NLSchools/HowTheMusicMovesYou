# How The Music Moves You

A single-file listening journal for Grade 9 STEAM, Beyond Our Planet, Lesson 8: Music and Space (Extension Activity). It replaces a Jamboard activity that no longer works.

The journal follows the activity's three parts:

- Part 1, What does it all mean: students watch the assigned "Rocket Man" by Elton John video (the animated film by Majid Adin, linked out to YouTube exactly as the lesson page links it), record at least three feelings or emotions on sticky notes, one per note, and answer the activity's three reflection questions.
- Part 2, Sounds that move you: students list and explain the five songs that make up their personal soundtrack, the songs that represent who they are and what they are about.
- Part 3, Art imitates life: students choose one of their five songs and illustrate it on the built-in drawing pad (or in any other medium, as the activity allows), and write about their illustration.

When they are done, the app compiles everything into one printable listening journal that they can print or copy into their portfolio. The journal ends with the activity's own reminder to take a break from the screen.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. The only external link is the assigned Rocket Man video. Single HTML file, works offline apart from that video link.
